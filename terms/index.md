## What Forge Fitness is

Forge Fitness is a training app for lifting and running. It records the
sets you lift and the runs you log, reads recovery data you choose to
connect, suggests your training, and shows you patterns in your own
numbers over time.

**The app is not a doctor, a physiotherapist, or a personal trainer, and
nothing in it is medical advice.** That includes the in-app coach, which

The app suggests training targets and describes how your recovery relates
to your training. Those suggestions are calculated from numbers you
logged, using published formulas and documented rules of thumb. They are
information, not instructions, and they cannot see you, your form, your
injuries, or your medical history.

- Talk to a qualified professional before starting or changing a training

## Friends, and what other people can see

The Friends leaderboard is optional. If you join it, other people who have
your friend code can see a display name you choose, weekly totals such as
sessions, volume, PR days and your streak, and progress toward your weekly
goals.

Recovery readings are never on that card. They are shared only with the
friends you choose, one friend at a time, as the Privacy Policy explains.
You can stop sharing at any time in Privacy & sharing, in the ☰ menu.

Do not use a display name, or post content, that identifies someone else
or that you would not want another user to see.
